Version 2.6.0.

* Accounts whose keys an organization manages can sign in and sync.
* Contacts without a signature have their own line, apart from failed signatures.
* An edit no longer re-signs a contact whose signature failed: it waits for your choice.
* Encryption keys and encrypted cards are checked more strictly.
* Edits and photos changed during a sync are no longer lost; large photos no longer stop the download.
