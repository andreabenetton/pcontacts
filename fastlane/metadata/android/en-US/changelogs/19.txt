Version 2.0.0.

Updating from 1.x? Sign in again once: the session is now kept with stronger, device-bound encryption and the old one cannot be carried over. The app says so on screen and in a notification.

* New single-screen layout with a sync status card and stepped sync interval.
* Import details from other apps' contacts (WhatsApp, Telegram, local…) into Proton. One-way: other apps' data never changes.
* See which apps can read your contacts.
* New "De-Googled Android ROMs" guide.
