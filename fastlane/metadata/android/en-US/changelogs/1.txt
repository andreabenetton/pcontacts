Initial release (0.1.0).

* SRP login with TOTP 2FA and modulus signature verification.
* Full contact sync: name, email, phone, address, organization,
  notes, instant messaging, photos, and group membership.
* Bidirectional sync with persistent outbox and push-before-pull.
* Per-field three-way conflict detection with resolution UI.
* Soft-delete with 1-hour grace period and cancellation support.
* Periodic sync (configurable interval) via SyncAdapter + WorkManager.
* Client-side-only OpenPGP decryption — no contact data leaves the device.
* No telemetry, no Google Play Services, no proprietary dependencies.
